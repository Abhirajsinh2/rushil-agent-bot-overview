Why most signals never became trades

Paper mode only — no real orders were placed. Fixed 1-share sizing (never scaled or compounded), so this net figure is not representative of capital-scaled returns. Entries and time-based exits fill at the next candle's open (not the decision candle's close) to avoid look-ahead bias; stop-loss, target, and forced square-off fill immediately. Time-based exit is rescaled to preserve Rushil's original ~120-minute hold intent at this candle granularity. Generated from journal_10m_short_intrabar/journal.csv.
