NIFTY 50 20-SMA Regime Filter

New entries gated by NIFTY 50's PREVIOUS completed daily candle, mirrored by direction: LONG needs that day's low > its 20-day SMA of close (clean uptrend, no touch); SHORT needs that day's high < its 20-day SMA (clean downtrend, no touch). 10-min candles only, full 2022-2026 history, trigger=intrabar throughout.

Result: mixed, not a clean win. Trade count dropped ~35-40% across all four combos as expected from a restrictive filter, and win rate rose for both LONG combos - but net P&L got WORSE for LONG, flipping the best single result from the whole 10-min sweep (+₹730.68, next-open) into a loss (−₹167.15). The filtered-out trades were apparently disproportionately the strategy's larger winners, not noise. SHORT losses shrank by roughly half but stayed negative. Read as: for this inside-bar breakout setup, a market already in a clean confirmed uptrend may be a worse regime than a choppier one, since compression/breakout patterns often thrive on consolidation rather than an already-extended trend.
