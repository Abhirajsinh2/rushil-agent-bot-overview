01 · Architecture

The agent pipeline

Every symbol is analyzed by the same five-agent chain each tick. Most of the system runs concurrently; exactly one step is serialized on purpose.

parallel (thread pool) serialized (one lock, no I/O under it) orchestrator wiring
DataAgent
Owns the broker feed only. Cache-first from data_cache/*.csv; falls back to a live Upstox pull. No indicators, no decisions.
IndicatorAgent
Turns the last 2 candles into a snapshot (inside-bar flag + OHLC). Never says BUY/SELL.
SignalAgent
The only place that knows the entry rules. LONG and SHORT tracked as separate strategy instances, each keyed by symbol+exchange+timeframe+strategy_id so they never collide.
RiskAgent serialized
Per-symbol checks, then the one account-wide atomic reservation step—no I/O held under the lock.
ExecutionDispatcher parallel
Priority queue, N workers, idempotent on client_order_id. Exits always outrank pending entries.

02 · Strategy

Trading logic

One setup, two independent directions, two configurable switches for how a breakout is confirmed and when it's assumed fillable.

close / next-open (original, no look-ahead) intrabar / same-candle (added this session)

Trigger & fill modes

TRIGGER = CLOSE

Confirmed on close

A bar's close crossing the range confirms the breakout. No look-ahead: the close is only known once the bar has finished.

TRIGGER = INTRABAR

Confirmed on cross

The range is broken the moment price trades past it (High/Low), like a resting breakout order. Entry buffer: min(0.03% of price, ₹2), gap-aware via the bar's open.

FILL = NEXT_OPEN

Filled next bar

Decided on candle N, filled at N+1's open. Stop stays fixed to its structural basis; distance/target and the 0.75% filter are re-derived from the real fill price.

FILL = SAME_CANDLE

Filled immediately

Fills within the triggering bar itself, at the buffer-adjusted price. Runs through the identical risk re-check as next-open—only the queue-and-wait step is skipped.

04 · This session

Bugs fixed

Both verified against the full 52-test suite before and after.

01

Cross-day inside-bar detection

The indicator agent compared the last two candles with no same-day guard—the first candle of a new session could be flagged "inside" yesterday's last candle. Fixed in indicator_agent.py with an explicit same-day check.

02

Stale setups surviving overnight

A pending range wasn't cleared at day boundaries, so a setup from yesterday's close could still trigger against today's open. Fixed in signal_agent.py by resetting pending state per key at the first snapshot of each new day.

03

O(n²) memory blowup in replay()

Each tick copied the entire candle history seen so far, though only the last 2 candles are ever read downstream—OOM'd the multi-year 5-min backtest. Fixed by bounding the slice to a 2-candle window.
